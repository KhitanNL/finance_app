PRIVACY

Wolfpack Run is a small hobby game for running clubs. We want to know roughly how many people play and where they come from, without tracking you.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Visits and game events: page views, races started and finished, results shared, clubs created and joined.
  • Rough location: country, region and city, worked out by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) from your connection. Never your exact location.
  • Where you came from: the website that linked you here (only its name, like whatsapp.com) and campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign).
  • Device type: mobile or desktop.
  • Date and hour of each event.

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • We never store your IP address.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or tracking storage on your device.
  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

HOW WE COUNT UNIQUE VISITORS

To count visitors we combine your IP address and browser type with a random value that changes every day, and turn that into a scrambled code (a hash). The IP address itself is thrown away straight away. Because the random value is deleted every day, the code from today cannot be linked to yesterday or tomorrow, and nobody can turn it back into your IP address.

PLAYERS, RUNNERS AND PHOTOS

In a club you are a player: you pick a player name (and optionally a small avatar). Your player name is shown on the leaderboards next to the runner you raced as, and your races, points and streaks belong to it. The runners are the club roster anyone can race as. A runner has a name, tagline, real-life personal bests, a short story and a photo, added by the club captain or by the real person after linking ("this is me").

Only upload a photo if you have permission from the person in it. Photos are cropped and resized on your phone and saved again as a new image, so camera data such as location (EXIF) is never uploaded; the server removes any that is left. Photos get a random file name.

Your phone keeps a private key in its own storage, so only you can change your player. You get a recovery code to open your player on a new phone. We only store a scrambled version (hash) of that code: no email address and no password. Anyone can report a runner photo, story or player avatar, and captains and the site admin can remove it.

RACE RESULTS

Finished races are saved with the runner, time and tap count so your club can have a fair leaderboard.

REMOVAL

You can change your player name and avatar at any time, and remove the photo or story of your own runner. Ask your club captain to remove your profile, or contact the site owner.
