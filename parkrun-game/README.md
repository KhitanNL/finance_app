# Wolfpack Run

An 8-bit mobile web game for the Zuiderpark Wolf group. Pick a runner card (Julian, Jordi, Luca, Sing, Ronald, Ivan, Jeroen, Dr Blake, Rody the diesel turtle, or secret runner Jony (Draakie)). Ren, Kipren and Sander are teased as "coming soon" (not playable yet), then run two laps of 2.5K
(5K total) that start and finish at the shimmering hall in Zuiderpark.

- Tap **RUN** as fast as you can. Faster tapping means a faster 5K time.
- Runner stats: a fibre mix of FAST and SLOW twitch (6 points). FAST = higher top speed, harder taps, stronger but shorter boosts, tires on lap 2. SLOW = holds speed, longer boosts, no lap 2 fatigue. Plus LOVE (heart boost) and JMP. Tap the ? on the runner screen for help.
- Tap **JUMP** (or the game screen) to grab boosts floating above the path:
  beetroot (faster for a while), Nomio shots (instant surge) and gels (every tap counts more).
- Jump over puddles and geese, they slow you down.
- Your PB per runner is saved on your phone.

Live at https://wolfpackrun.app. Or open `index.html` in a phone browser. It is a single file with no build step.
Runner names, taglines, perks, stats, kit colours and rival target times live in the `RUNNERS` array near the top of the script. Card photos are in `img/`.
